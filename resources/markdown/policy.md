This Privacy Policy explains how **BluePin Inc** ("BluePin", "we", "us", "our"), the company responsible for Dot.HR, collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you use Dot.HR and the wider Dot Ecosystem it connects to. It is written to align with South Africa's **Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA")**.

Dot.HR is a workforce management platform, and it handles two different kinds of personal information. This Policy explains both.

## 1. Who we are

BluePin Inc is the responsible party for the personal information described in this Policy. Our Information Officer can be reached at privacy@infodot.co.za for any question, request, or concern about your personal information.

## 2. Two kinds of personal information Dot.HR handles

- **Your account information**, if you sign in to Dot.HR to manage a team's employment records — here, BluePin is the **responsible party**, and this Policy governs how we handle it.
- **Employment records**, if your employer uses Dot.HR to record your role, employment status, or leave — here, **your employer (the team that manages Dot.HR)** is the responsible party for what they record about you, and BluePin acts as an **operator**, processing that data only on the team's instructions, to run the platform they use. Employees don't need their own Dot.HR login for a record to exist about them — an employment record can exist independently of any account. If you have a question about your own employment record, contact your employer first.

## 3. What we collect (account holders)

**Account information** — your name, email address, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text), via your Dot Ecosystem account.

**Team information** — the team you belong to and your role within it (e.g. admin or member), used to control who can view or edit employment records.

**Technical information** — IP address, browser and device information, and session activity, collected automatically for security and to keep you signed in.

## 4. What we process on a team's behalf (employment records)

Dot.HR is deliberately built to keep the personal information it holds about employees to a minimum. For each employment record, we process:

- **Position** — job title and role description. This describes work, not a person.
- **Employee** — name, work email or phone, position, employment type, status, and start/end date. We do not collect national ID or passport numbers, salary, or medical information, and the platform is deliberately not designed to hold them.
- **Leave requests** — the dates and status of a leave request, along with a free-text reason field. We treat this reason field as potentially sensitive, since an employee may voluntarily disclose health-related information in it, even though the field isn't designed to require that.

We process this data only as instructed by the team that manages it — we don't use it for our own purposes, and we don't sell it.

## 5. Work, not workers

Dot.HR's core design principle is that it publishes and shares knowledge about work — roles, structures, schedules — never knowledge that scores, ranks, or profiles an individual employee. Employment records are excluded, at a structural level, from any shared or aggregated data set. As of this Policy's date, Dot.HR has no automated data-sharing pipeline to any other system at all — no employment record leaves the platform except through the team's own direct use of it (viewing, editing, or exporting their own team's data).

## 6. Why we process your information

We process personal information to:

- create and maintain your account, and authenticate you when you sign in;
- let you sign in once and move between connected Dot Ecosystem platforms without re-entering your credentials;
- let a team record positions, employment records, and leave requests, scoped to that team; and
- keep Dot.HR secure and prevent unauthorised access.

## 7. Ecosystem single sign-on

When you use another Dot Ecosystem platform to sign in to Dot.HR (or vice versa), a short-lived, single-use authentication token confirms who you are without exposing your password to the connected platform.

## 8. Who can see employment records

Employment records are scoped to the team that manages them. Within a team, viewing positions, employment records, and leave requests is available to any team member; creating, editing, or deleting them requires an admin role or team ownership. Employment records are never visible outside the team that owns them, and are never shared with other teams or platforms.

## 9. How long we keep your information

We keep account and employment data for as long as your account or the relevant employment record is active. If a team removes an employment record, or you delete your account, the associated data is removed, except where we're required by law to retain certain records for longer.

## 10. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted password storage, team- and role-scoped access control, and single-use SSO tokens for ecosystem sign-in. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

## 11. Your rights under POPIA

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

- request access to the personal information we hold about you;
- request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
- request deletion of your personal information;
- object to our processing of your personal information in certain circumstances; and
- lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

If your request concerns an employment record, we may need to direct you to your employer, since they are the responsible party for that data. To exercise any of these rights regarding your Dot.HR account, contact privacy@infodot.co.za.

## 12. Cookies

Dot.HR uses a session cookie to keep you signed in. See our Cookie Policy for details.
