# Dot.HR — Complete Product Transformation, Workforce Ecosystem & World-Class UX/UI

> Owner brief, received 2026-10-31. Stored verbatim as requirement R-32.

## 1. The Vision: Build More Than an HR Platform

Let's now focus on transforming Dot.HR into one of the most advanced, intuitive and comprehensive employment and workforce management platforms available.

I do not want Dot.HR to become just another recruitment website where employers post vacancies and candidates upload CVs.

I want Dot.HR to become a complete employment ecosystem connecting businesses registered on InfoDot with individuals across the Dot ecosystem.

The platform must support the entire employment journey, from discovering opportunities and recruiting talent to onboarding, managing employees, developing careers, processing workforce activities and managing the employee lifecycle.

**Dot.HR should become the place where businesses find people, people find meaningful work, and both parties manage their professional relationship.**

The experience must be seamless for employers and individuals.

Businesses should be able to find the right people, recruit them, onboard them and manage their workforce without unnecessary administrative complexity.

Individuals should be able to establish professional profiles, discover opportunities, apply for work, communicate with employers, manage employment documents, track applications and develop their careers.

Do not play small when it comes to functionality.

Study the world's leading recruitment, HR, workforce management, talent management and professional development platforms. Identify their strongest capabilities and opportunities to create a substantially better experience.

Consider products such as LinkedIn, Indeed, Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, BambooHR, Rippling, Deel, Gusto, SAP SuccessFactors, Zoho Recruit and other relevant platforms.

Do not simply copy them. Build a distinctive Dot.HR experience that combines the best ideas into a coherent ecosystem.

Our ambition is to make Dot.HR a serious international product that can serve individuals, small businesses, growing companies and large enterprises.

## 2. The Foundation: InfoDot Business and User Identity

Dot.HR must be deeply integrated with the InfoDot identity and business ecosystem.

InfoDot should remain the central identity and authentication gateway.

### Business integration

Businesses registered on InfoDot should be able to access Dot.HR using their existing business identity.

A business should not have to register its company again just to use Dot.HR.

Where supported by the actual InfoDot integration, Dot.HR should retrieve:

- Registered business identity.
- Trading name.
- Business registration details.
- Verification status.
- Business logo and profile imagery.
- Business description.
- Industry and business category.
- Locations and operating regions.
- Authorised representatives.
- Existing business team members.
- Relevant business contact information.

Business verification must be represented accurately.

A business being registered on InfoDot must not automatically mean every person claiming to represent it is authorised to recruit or manage employees.

Implement business-level permissions and authorised recruitment roles.

### Individual integration

Individuals should use their existing InfoDot identity to access Dot.HR.

Their professional profile should become a reusable identity within the Dot ecosystem.

Where appropriate and authorised, individuals should be able to reuse:

- Personal profile information.
- Professional information.
- Skills and qualifications.
- Employment history.
- Certifications.
- Portfolio information.
- Existing ecosystem activity and achievements.

Never expose private information from another Dot platform without explicit permission and an appropriate integration.

### Single identity, multiple professional relationships

An individual may simultaneously be:

- An employee at one company.
- A contractor for another company.
- A freelancer serving multiple clients.
- A business owner.
- A job seeker.
- A recruiter or hiring manager.

The platform must support these different relationships without requiring multiple accounts.

A user should be able to switch between their individual professional workspace and authorised business workspaces.
